City of Harborview
Harborview Transit and Community Services Board
Regular Meeting Agenda
June 24, 2026

Item 1: Public Transit Funding Review
Receive a staff report on proposed allocation of regional mobility funds for bus frequency improvements, discounted youth passes, and expanded evening service in neighborhoods with lower vehicle ownership. Staff recommends approval of a funding plan and requests direction on community outreach before final adoption.

Item 2: Public Safety Update
The Police Department and Fire Department will provide a quarterly public safety update, including emergency response times, pedestrian injury trends near transit corridors, and proposed coordination with behavioral health outreach teams.

Item 3: Housing Services Grant
Consider Resolution No. 2026-18 authorizing a $2.4 million grant agreement with nonprofit community-based organizations to provide homelessness prevention, tenant navigation, emergency rental assistance, and supportive housing referrals.

Item 4: Accessibility Improvements
Approve an Americans with Disabilities Act transition plan update for bus stops, curb ramps, digital notices, and public meeting materials. The plan includes accessible formats, multilingual notices, and a public comment period.

Item 5: Community Engagement Report
Receive the annual community engagement report summarizing workshops, surveys, language access needs, and recommendations to improve participation by immigrant communities, LGBTQ+ residents, seniors, youth, and people with disabilities.
